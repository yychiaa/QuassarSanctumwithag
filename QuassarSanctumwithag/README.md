# Quassar Sanctum
An interactive forensic science investigation game set within an isolated oceanic research observatory after a catastrophic containment incident. The player assumes the role of a lead field investigator sent to reconstruct what happened inside the abandoned facility, working alongside fellow agents while navigating its sectors, examining physical evidence, recovering scientific records, accessing classified archives, and interpreting fragmented telemetry left behind by the research team. Rather than revealing its story through a single linear narrative, the game allows the mystery of Quassar Observatory to emerge through investigation: each location, document, specimen, system log, and communication contributes another piece to the events surrounding ACF-01, the Acoustic Resonance Chamber, Project KHYRA, and the final containment operation. The experience combines environmental storytelling, scientific-fiction worldbuilding, evidence-based progression, and an investigative interface to place the player inside the reconstruction of the incident rather than simply telling them what occurred.
Quassar Sanctum aims to create a narrative experience in which exploration, observation, and critical interpretation become essential parts of understanding the story. The project is designed to encourage players to connect information from multiple sources, distinguish relevant evidence from surrounding data, reconstruct a chronology from incomplete records, and gradually develop an understanding of the Quassar incident before reaching its final operational resolution. By integrating forensic investigation mechanics with interactive archives, evidence tracking, environmental exploration, agent communications, and scientific-fiction storytelling, the project seeks to demonstrate how a web-based game can make narrative discovery active rather than passive. Ultimately, Quassar Sanctum is intended not only as a mystery to be completed, but as a world that must be investigated, reconstructed, and understood through the evidence it leaves behind.

## Features
- 7 interactive investigation sectors
- Dynamic inspection system
- Relational Archive
- Evidence tracking
- Agent Sync
- Procedural Web Audio
- Multiple endings

## Technology

- Vanilla JavaScript
